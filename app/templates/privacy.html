Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: September 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how TrendBlogo ("we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, processes, stores, and safeguards personal information when you visit our website, interact with our content, submit guest posting proposals, or transmit inquiries through our contact forms. We are committed to transparency and adherence to applicable global data protection regulations including GDPR and CCPA.

2. Categories of Information Collected

We collect information across the following categories:

  • Directly Provided Information: Your name, email address, website URL, article outlines, and message content when submitting contact or guest posting forms.
  • System Log Data: Technical identifiers including your IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, referring URLs, timestamps, and page request telemetry automatically generated by web servers.
  • Operational Cookies: Session tokens and preference identifiers used for authentication, security verification, and performance analysis.

3. Artificial Intelligence and API Processing

TrendBlogo utilizes server-side AI language and visual generation APIs (such as OpenAI models) to assist in article compilation, outline structuring, and image generation. We do not transmit user personal data to third-party AI training models. Keywords, outlines, and prompts sent to AI generation services are processed strictly via secure enterprise endpoints subject to standard zero-retention or transient processing agreements.

4. Use of Collected Data

We process collected data exclusively for the following operational purposes:

  • To deliver, maintain, and optimize platform content and services;
  • To review, evaluate, and correspond regarding guest posting proposals;
  • To respond to user inquiries and technical support requests;
  • To prevent automated spam, brute-force security threats, and fraudulent submissions;
  • To monitor aggregated site performance and search indexation telemetry.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data with vetted service providers operating under strict confidentiality obligations, including cloud hosting providers, database infrastructure services, and security firewalls.

6. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure. Contact messages and guest post proposals are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill their editorial purpose, after which they are archived or deleted.

7. User Rights and Data Control

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate records, request deletion of your information, or withdraw consent for non-essential cookies. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at our official support email.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our architecture or changes in statutory requirements. Revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective timestamp.
