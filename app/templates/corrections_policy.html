Accountability & Accuracy

Corrections & Updates Policy

Our protocol for correcting errors promptly, transparently disclosing updates, and preserving historical record.

1. Our Commitment to Accuracy

At TrendBlogo, accuracy is the bedrock of our reporting. When factual errors occur—whether in hardware benchmark charts, code snippets, company valuations, or technical explanations—our newsroom commits to correcting them transparently, thoroughly, and without delay.

2. How Corrections Are Handled

When an error is brought to our attention and verified by our editorial staff:

  • Factual Corrections: If an article contains an incorrect specification, benchmark score, or quotation, the text is amended and an explicit Correction Notice is appended to the bottom or top of the article detailing what was changed and the timestamp of the correction.
  • Developing Tech Stories: For breaking technology stories, we append an Update Note with the date and time when new disclosures or manufacturer responses become available.
  • Typographical Edits: Minor spelling or grammatical edits that do not alter the factual meaning of a sentence may be corrected quietly without a formal notice.

3. Reporting an Error or Erratum

We encourage readers, developers, and industry practitioners to hold us to the highest standard. If you discover a factual mistake or broken benchmark in any TrendBlogo article, please notify our corrections team:

Corrections Desk Email:

corrections@trendblogo.com

Please include the article URL, the specific passage in question, and any supporting documentation or benchmark logs to expedite verification.
