Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does TrendBlogo prevent generic AI content?

TrendBlogo divides content generation into specialized steps: intent analysis, anti-cannibalization check, structured outline planning, section synthesis, visual asset creation, link injection, and quality auditing. This guarantees deep topical relevance and human-readable quality.

Why is having zero hyperlinks in headings strictly enforced?

Headings (H2-H5) serve as foundational semantic anchors for search engines and screen-reading assistive technologies. Placing hyperlinks in headings degrades accessibility and harms topical entity extraction. TrendBlogo guarantees that headings remain pure plain text while placing links in body sentences.

How does the 4-image generation system work?

Each generated article receives exactly 1 featured hero image plus 3 contextually tailored in-article images placed throughout major sections. The system dynamically creates unique prompts based on section headings and renders crisp, responsive visual assets.

Can I review and edit articles before they go live?

Yes. The admin console includes a full WYSIWYG and Markdown editor, heading hierarchy inspector, image replacement tools, and instant Save Draft / Schedule / Publish controls.
