Editorial Philosophy & What We Accept

TrendBlogo welcomes in-depth, deeply technical, and analytically grounded articles authored by engineers, practitioners, SEO strategists, and technology leaders. We prioritize original frameworks, reproducible methodologies, and actionable benchmarks over superficial commentary.

Allowed Core Topics

  • AI content generation pipelines and LLM orchestration
  • Architectural SEO, structured data, and search intent clustering
  • Modern workflow automation and developer productivity
  • Digital content syndication and analytics telemetry
  • API development and automated web publishing infrastructure

Strictly Prohibited Content

  • Unedited, raw AI text dumps lacking structured formatting
  • Promotional press releases or veiled affiliate listicles
  • Spam niches (gambling, payday loans, crypto speculation)
  • Spun, plagiarized, or duplicate content
  • Articles with forced anchor links placed inside headings

Strict Linking & Heading Policy

In alignment with TrendBlogo's accessibility and SEO standards, never place hyperlinks inside H2, H3, H4, or H5 headings. Headings must remain plain text only. Contextual internal and external links must appear naturally inside body paragraphs. We permit up to 1 relevant link to your verified personal website or documentation resource within the author bio.
