Understand How We Use Cookies & Manage Your Preferences

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files stored on your device by your web browser when you access websites. They allow platforms to recognize your browser, persist user preferences, ensure session security, and aggregate anonymized performance telemetry.

Cookie Preference Center

You can customize your cookie consent settings below. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are required for fundamental site operation.

Essential & Security Cookies

Required for secure authentication, CSRF validation, and session management. Cannot be switched off.

Always Active

Functional Preferences

Analytics Telemetry

Collects anonymized traffic patterns and reading dwell times to improve content quality.

Managing Cookies via Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to modify cookie handling through their settings menu. Note that restricting essential cookies may degrade platform authentication and navigation functionality.
